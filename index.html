Scientific Grounding & Attribution

NASA Open Data Used in This Mission

This trainer distinguishes live NASA API telemetry from curated NASA mission baselines & training drills—so static numbers are never presented as live.

Checking live NASA DONKI telemetry...
1. NASA DONKI (Space Weather Database)
Live queries to api.nasa.gov/DONKI (CMEAnalysis, Notifications, GST Kp Index, SEP Solar Particle Events). Scales Stage 4 solar storm intensity and distinguishes live solar weather from simulated training drills.
2. NASA OSDR (Open Science Data Repository)
Curated astronaut dosimetry & biological guidelines: 600 mSv NASA career radiation threshold, shielding trade-offs, and closed-loop plant/water recycling studies used in Crew Safety and Habitat Science.
3. Mars Surface Missions (Curiosity RAD, Perseverance MEDA & MOXIE, InSight SEIS)
Curated Mars surface baselines: ~0.66 mSv/day radiation background (RAD), 600–750 Pa thin 95% CO₂ air at Jezero Crater (MEDA), CO₂/ice Sabatier methane synthesis (ISRU), 24.6 hr Sol cycle, 4–24 min one-way light-speed communication delay, and marsquake monitoring (InSight SEIS).
